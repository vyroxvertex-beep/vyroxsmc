27. Decisions: approved and locked

  • Images JPG, PNG, WEBP; documents PDF only; 10 MB per file; access for enrolled students, mentors and admins.
  • Refunds need admin approval. Requests before course access, and after access by policy-based review. Exact eligibility, cutoffs and exceptions are not defined here.
  • Duplicate payments: idempotency key, reconciliation, refund workflow.
  • Device changes: admin approval only; pending requests expire after 24 hours.
  • Mute: 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days, permanent. Muted students cannot post in group chat or doubt sessions; reading stays.
  • Tablet navigation expanded by default; Mentorship, Groups and Doubts have separate icons.
  • Payment gateway generic; provider pending.
  • Logo: original vector or PNG of at least 512 × 512 px, no distortion.
  • One active device per account; old device stays active during a pending request and is revoked only after admin approval; every approval and change audit-logged; suspicious sign-ins recorded as security events.

32a. Fonts

  • Inter for English and Tamil Tanglish (Latin script).
  • Noto Sans Tamil for Tamil script, with Inter as the Latin fallback inside the same stack.

  • This preview loads both fonts from Google Fonts. Self-hosting for the real app is a technical choice to be approved later.
